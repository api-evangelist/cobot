# Cobot

> Cobot is the most user-friendly and intuitive coworking space management software, trusted by coworking spaces worldwide. It offers a fully localized platform, best-in-class customer support, and all the features needed to run a coworking space efficiently.

Cobot is an all-in-one coworking space management solution offering powerful features for bookings, invoicing, membership plans, integrations, analytics, and access control. Its intuitive interface and strong localization support make it ideal for teams worldwide. Cobot is trusted by hundreds of spaces across the globe and is known for having the best-rated support in the industry.

## Core Features

- **Mobile App**: Fully-featured mobile app for space admins and members, available on Android and iOS. The app is white-labeled, allowing coworking spaces to showcase their own branding, colors, and logo.
- **Automated Billing**: Automated invoicing and online payments with flexible billing options and integration with major payment providers
- **Smart Bookings**: Conflict-free booking calendars with real-time availability and Google Calendar sync
- **Member Portal**: White-label member portal with self-service features, branding, and community directory
- **Event Management**: Event and visitor booking tools with public access and flexible pricing
- **Access Control**: Access control integrations with systems like Kisi, SaltoKS, Tapkey, and Sensorberg
- **Analytics**: Analytics and reporting dashboards with CSV export for revenue, usage, and member metrics
- **Multi-location**: Multi-location support and open API for advanced customization and integrations
- **Security**: GDPR-compliant data handling, secure infrastructure, and legal readiness for global operations

## About Cobot

Cobot was founded over 15 years ago by passionate coworking enthusiasts who originally built the platform to automate and manage their own coworking space, and it remains independently operated. The company is based in Germany and Canada, with an experienced international team supporting spaces across the globe.

- Developed and hosted in Germany, operating under strict EU data protection regulations
- 3 co-founders, including one female founder
- Member and active participant in numerous coworking and community organizations
- Learn more: [About Cobot](https://www.cobot.me/en/about)

We are committed to building a better, more inclusive coworking ecosystem:

- Cobot maintains a [Code of Conduct](https://www.cobot.me/en/community-responsibility) and regularly contributes to discussions on inclusion, accessibility, and ethical technology. We actively support the principles of **IDEA**—Inclusion, Diversity, Equity, and Accessibility.
